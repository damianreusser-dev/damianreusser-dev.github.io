Privacy policy – History Meta Reach

Last updated: 31 October 2026

History Meta Reach is an internal, read-only analytics tool used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel The History Meta. It is not offered to the public.

What data the tool accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the tool uses Google's OAuth sign-in and these read-only permissions:

  • yt-analytics.readonly – aggregated YouTube Analytics and reporting data for the owner's own channel (for example impressions, click-through rate, views, watch time, traffic sources and audience retention).
  • youtube.readonly – public details of the owner's videos and of public videos that appear as traffic sources (title, channel name, category).

The tool does not access personal data of individual viewers. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual people.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner how their own videos are discovered and watched, so they can improve titles, thumbnails and topics. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with third parties.

Storage

Data and the sign-in token are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and in the owner's private records. Nothing is published.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be sent via the contact options on the The History Meta channel page.
