History Meta Reach

An internal analytics tool for the YouTube channel The History Meta.

History Meta Reach is a small script used only by the owner of The History Meta. It signs in with the channel owner's own Google account and reads that channel's YouTube Analytics (impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources, audience retention) so the owner can see how each video is discovered and decide how to improve titles, thumbnails and topics.

The tool is read-only. It cannot upload, edit, comment, or change anything on YouTube, and it is not offered to other users.
